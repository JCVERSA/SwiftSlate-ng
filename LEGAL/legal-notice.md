## 5. Use of artificial intelligence — transparency

1. To run a command, SwiftSlate sends the text the user has explicitly selected to a **third-party AI model provider**. That provider is **chosen and configured by the user**, who supplies their own API key.
2. Returned text is **generated by an artificial intelligence system**. It may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or unsuited to the context. The publisher does not control or verify it and does not guarantee its accuracy or fitness for any purpose.
3. The user remains solely responsible for how generated text is used, including verifying it before any professional, administrative, legal, medical or commercial use.
4. When no provider is configured, or when the application's **privacy mode** is enabled, no text is transmitted: only local text-replacement commands, executed entirely on the device, remain available.
5. Data sent to a third-party provider is then governed by that provider's privacy policy. Provider-by-provider detail is set out in the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

## 9. Points to watch

These items are not current shortcomings, but they **would change the obligations described above**:

| Event | Consequence |
|:--|:--|
| Publishing the app on Google Play | The store listing imposes its own requirements (privacy policy reachable at a public URL, Data safety form), independently of the LCEN |
| Launching a website (landing page, docs) | The legal notice becomes mandatory on the website itself, on the same terms |
| Donations or sponsorships becoming regular or significant | The activity may be reclassified as professional: the Article 6-III-2 LCEN anonymity falls away, and identification duties (company number, etc.) may arise |
| Registering as a sole trader or a company | The legal notice must state the company name, legal form, registered office, registration number, share capital and VAT number where applicable |
| Adding a server, user accounts, analytics or crash reporting | New data processing appears: the privacy policy and the register must be updated **before** go-live |
